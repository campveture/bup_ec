# BUPEC visual direction — The Open Ledger

## Intent

Make the site feel like a living student publication and an event archive. Use documentary images, strong type, and designed white space to lead visitors through people, programmes, and evidence.

## Fixed brand system

- Keep the existing navy, teal, cyan, mist, and paper palette from `website/app/globals.css`.
- Use Fraunces for expressive headlines and Inter for reading, navigation, and data.
- Keep original sponsor marks recognizable; give them breathing room instead of recoloring them.

## Composition

- Combine full-width photographs with asymmetrical editorial grids.
- Use large edition numerals, fine rules, angled image frames, and archival captions.
- Let event photos and member portraits carry the visual weight. Put concise facts beside them.
- Show a specific year or edition wherever an archival photo appears.

## Image handling

- Keep people and event identities faithful to the source photos. Do not generate or replace real faces.
- Edit image presentation through masks, crops, overlays, responsive framing, and captions in the site.
- Save originals locally before replacing the remote URLs. The 25 supplied panel cards are already in `website/public/panel/`.
- `website/public/editorial-landscape.png` is original abstract artwork, used only as a graphic backdrop. It is not presented as an event photograph.

## Interaction

- Use subtle reveal motion and hover changes, with reduced-motion support.
- Keep links and controls legible, keyboard accessible, and consistent across page templates.
